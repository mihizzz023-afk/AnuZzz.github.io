Top 5 Telegram Privacy Tricks You Should Enable Right Now

Five practical privacy settings to review if you want more control over your Telegram account, visibility and conversations.

📅 September 23, 2026 ◷ 5 min read 🔐 Privacy Guide

Telegram includes several settings that let you control who can see parts of your profile and activity, who can contact you, and how your app is protected on your device. Here are five useful settings worth checking.

In this guide 1. Hide Last Seen & Online Status 2. Hide Your Phone Number 3. Use Secret Chats 4. Control Group & Channel Invitations 5. Lock Telegram with a Passcode
Privacy Trick 01

Hide Your Last Seen & Online Status

Telegram lets you choose who can see your Last Seen information. The available basic choices include Everybody, My Contacts and Nobody, with additional exceptions available.

How to do it:
Go to Settings → Privacy and Security → Last Seen & Online and choose the audience you want.
Telegram also applies Last Seen privacy rules to Online status, although there are some brief exceptions when certain actions make your online activity visible.
Privacy Trick 02

Hide Your Phone Number

You can control who can see your phone number through Telegram's privacy settings. This is useful if you prefer to communicate without displaying your number to everyone you interact with.

How to do it:
Go to Settings → Privacy and Security → Phone Number and select the privacy option that suits you.
If another person already knows your phone number and has saved it in their contacts, changing this setting does not make them forget a number they already know.
Privacy Trick 03

Use Secret Chats for End-to-End Encryption

Telegram Secret Chats use end-to-end encryption. Telegram states that only the two participants can read messages in a Secret Chat.

How to do it:
Open the person's profile, open the menu, and choose Start Secret Chat when the option is available.
Secret Chats are different from normal Cloud Chats. They are tied to the devices and sessions where they were created.
Privacy Trick 04

Control Who Can Add You to Groups & Channels

Telegram provides privacy controls for who can add you to groups and channels. You can review this setting and use the available audience and exception controls.

How to check it:
Go to Settings → Privacy and Security → Groups & Channels and review who is allowed to add you.
Privacy Trick 05

Lock Telegram with a Passcode

Telegram has a built-in Passcode Lock. When a passcode is enabled, a lock icon appears above the chat list and can be used to lock the app.

How to do it:
Go to Settings → Privacy and Security → Passcode Lock and enable the feature.
A device-level screen lock is also important. App protection and account protection work best when both are enabled.

Quick Privacy Checklist 🔐

  • Review who can see your Last Seen & Online status.
  • Check who can see your phone number.
  • Use Secret Chats when end-to-end encryption is appropriate.
  • Review who can add you to groups and channels.
  • Enable Telegram's Passcode Lock.
Information checked against Telegram's official documentation.
